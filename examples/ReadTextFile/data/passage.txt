The last train leaves after sunset. At the main entrance, turn right and take the lift to the footbridge. The lift has an audio button on the lower panel. Press it once to hear the platform number, or hold it down to repeat the directions.

On the other side of the bridge, follow the handrail to the waiting room. The café beside it closes at seven. There is a drinking fountain just inside the door, and a shelter on the platform if you would rather wait outside.

If the platform changes, listen for the next announcement. Staff can also help at the ticket desk.
